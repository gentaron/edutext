Purpose
The name first encountered in a story is not necessarily a formal first name.
It may be a familiar form, alias, surname address or inherited public name.
Mina is a familiar form of Wilhelmina; the formal first name is Wilhelmina.
This revision withdraws the previous treatment of Mina as the first name with a new nickname, Mina/Miina, the mechanical splitting of name positions, and forced new pet names for everyone.

Naming decisions
- User instructions take priority: Wilhelmina Eureka Ernest is Mina's formal name.
- Existing story calls remain usable. If they are familiar forms, supply the formal name and place the existing call in the familiar-name field.
- Keep natural independent given names, including Layla, Iris, Leon and Karin, without forcing longer forms.
- Use real naming relationships where appropriate, such as Katherine→Kate, Isaac→Ike, Thomas→Tom and Friedrich→Fritz.
  A familiar form can have several possible expansions; the chosen fictional name is not asserted to be its unique etymology.
- Accept local fictional names as names. Casteria remains Casteria; Cass is an in-world short form from Cas-. Withdraw the previous カス.
- Separate aliases from linguistic diminutives. Alpha, Goldilocks and White Noise receive distinct formal identities.
- Supply missing human personal/family names as creative worldbuilding, with a coherent naming style.
  Divine, nonhuman and other-civilization mononyms need not acquire human surnames.
- Assign middle names only where useful; do not add them mechanically to Japanese names or mononyms.
- An em dash means no separate element is adopted in this naming setting. It is not a research placeholder.
  Where no nickname is listed, use the formal given name or the existing public name.
- Do not create nicknames merely by arbitrary clipping, adding Japanese honorifics, or appending generation numbers.
- Display names in their appropriate order; Japanese formal displays use family→given order.
- Preserve distinctions between birth names, public names and inherited identities. Do not invent replacement identities for crossover characters.
- A language-style choice does not establish an undocumented birthplace, nationality, ethnicity or family relationship.

Setting status
User specified: explicitly directed in this conversation.
Retained: existing given names adopted as formal names; optional familiar forms do not expand those names.
New completion: includes formal names, additional names, surnames or familiar-name relationships newly adopted here.
Reclassified: corrected roles or order of name elements; any creative completion is explained individually.
Crossover retained: keeps the source character's name and withdraws the earlier added nickname.

Sources and spelling
Narrative sources identify characters and their earlier calls. They are not evidence that the newly supplied full identities already appeared in those files.
This reference adopts creative naming settings as requested by the user; it does not present all names as recovered canon.
Individual notes give full names and reasons. Latin spellings are selected in this reference; an unestablished Japanese surname reading remains in kanji.
Existing story dialogue and public names are not globally replaced.

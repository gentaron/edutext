Purpose
A separate reference for first names, middle names, familiar nicknames and last names, supporting consistent dialogue.
Name values retain the original source script to avoid inventing undocumented romanizations.
The Japanese and English editions contain the same entries and nicknames.

Conventions
1. New means a familiar nickname assigned in this reference. Existing means an attested familiar form adopted from the text.
2. A nickname is not part of a formal name. Use established everyday names in ordinary dialogue, these nicknames among close acquaintances, and formal names or established names with titles in official settings.
   Retain generation labels when necessary to distinguish inherited names.
3. Not recorded means the referenced text does not explicitly supply that element; it does not assert that no such element exists.
   A mononym is placed in the First / used-name field without asserting that it is a birth name.
4. Multi-part names are provisionally arranged as first / central / final elements following source order.
   This editorial split does not establish legal naming roles or family relationships where the source is silent.
   Japanese family/given names are separated; フォン (von) and アル＝ are retained with family-name elements.
5. Similar names do not establish identical people. Variants and inherited names are explained in notes.
   ミユシャリ and ファリエル are two people; the grouped wording ミユシャリ・ファリエル is not treated as one full name.
6. Existing hero names and titles remain available alongside familiar nicknames, including Layla's Pink Voltage.
7. Organizations, states, facilities, ships, objects, generic species, unnamed extras and references to real people are excluded.
   ボグダス・ジャベリン, SSレンジ and ドミニオン・ヴァーミリオン are organization or territory names, not personal names.
   Crossover characters have a separate section.
8. Sources support names and attested forms, not prior usage of newly assigned nicknames.
   This reference supports future writing; it does not replace existing names or dialogue in story files.
